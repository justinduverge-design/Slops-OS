# Mission: Evolve the "Slops Saloon" identity into a sophisticated, mythological-themed platform for the "Slops" OS.

1) Name Quest — platform-name proposals (concise rationale)

- Slops Archive — evokes the Library of Alexandria: a curated, authoritative repository for signals, lore, and historical trades.
- Slops Citadel — a defensible, institutional face: authority, governance, and a civic center for protocol decisions (historical parallel: Byzantium/Citadel cities).
- Slops Agora — positions the platform as a market and civic forum (parallel: Athenian Agora), ideal for discovery and community debate.
- Slops Nexus — modern, connective: an intersection for liquidity, integrations, and third‑party apps (parallel: trade nexuses like Constantinople).
- Slops Pantheon — elevates contributors, strategies and heroes into a mythic hall of fame; good for prestige and recognition.
- The Slops Athenaeum — scholarly; positions the product as the knowledge hub and research center for the OS.

Recommendation: primary brand = "Slops Nexus" (broad, extensible), with "Slops Saloon" preserved as the local tavern/press inside the Nexus.

# GTM Strategy

Create a comprehensive go-to-market strategy for a product launch — channels, messaging, success metrics, and launch timeline. Input via `$ARGUMENTS`.

## When to Use

Planning a product launch, creating a GTM plan from scratch, defining a launch strategy for a new market, developing product-to-market-fit strategy, preparing a go-live roadmap.

## How It Works

**Step 1 — Gather research**: product description & key features, target segment details, market research/validation, competitive landscape, customer interviews/survey data.

**Step 2 — Define channels**: digital (paid search, social, display); content/inbound (blog, SEO, thought leadership); sales/outbound (direct outreach, partnerships); community/grassroots; product-led/viral.

**Step 3 — Develop messaging**: core value prop for segment, key differentiators, pain-point→solution mapping, proof points/social proof, channel-specific variations.

**Step 4 — Define success metrics**: awareness (impressions, reach, recall); engagement (CTR, CPE, time on site); conversion (signups, demos, trials); revenue (MRR, CAC, LTV); market (share, segment penetration).

**Step 5 — Create launch plan**: pre-launch prep, launch-day activities, post-launch momentum (content, partnerships, communities), measurement/optimization cadence, go/no-go decision points.

## Output

Structured GTM doc: recommended channels with justification, channel-specific messaging/positioning, launch timeline with milestones, KPI targets & measurement framework, risk mitigation, 90-day execution roadmap.

## Tips

Start with the most confident segment; validate assumptions via interviews before full launch; do a few channels excellently rather than many poorly; baseline metrics before launch; build feedback loops.

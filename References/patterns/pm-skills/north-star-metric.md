# North Star Metric

Identify a North Star Metric (NSM) and 3-5 Input Metrics that form a metrics constellation. Classify the business game and validate the NSM against criteria for effectiveness. Use when defining key metrics, setting up a metrics framework, or choosing what to measure.

## Domain Context

NSM is **NOT**: multiple metrics, a revenue/LTV metric (must be customer-centric), an OKR (goal-setting), or a strategy (though choosing the right NSM is strategic).

NSM **IS**: a single, customer-centric KPI reflecting the value customers get, serving as a leading indicator of long-term success. Use OKRs to express expected change in the NSM.

## The Three Business Games

Classify first:

- **Attention** — how much time customers spend (Facebook, Spotify, YouTube, TikTok).
- **Transaction** — how many transactions occur (Amazon, Uber, Airbnb, PayPal).
- **Productivity** — how efficiently someone completes work/goals (Canva, Dropbox, Loom, Notion).

## Prompt

Given business context `$ARGUMENTS`:

**Step 1 — Classify the game**: Attention, Transaction, or Productivity.

**Step 2 — Identify the NSM** meeting all 7 criteria:
1. Easy to understand. 2. Customer-centric. 3. Sustainable value (habits, long-term engagement). 4. Vision alignment. 5. Quantitative. 6. Actionable. 7. Leading indicator.

**Step 3 — Identify 3-5 Input Metrics** (leading indicators) that most directly drive the NSM. Each should be easier to move short-term, directly contribute to the NSM, and focus optimization effort.

## Tips

Provide business/revenue model, vision/mission, current metrics, key segments and use cases, and the primary value delivered.

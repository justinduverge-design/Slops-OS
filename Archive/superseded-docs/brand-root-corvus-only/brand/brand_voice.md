# Brand Voice

This file defines how Corvus documents, product language, and agent-generated operating content should sound.

---

## Voice Standard

Corvus documents are written in a voice that is:

- institutional but human
- confident
- precise
- direct
- opinionated
- serious
- built to win

The tone should feel like a disciplined organization, not a corporation pretending to have personality.

---

## Corvus Product Voice

Corvus is the active product name as of 2026-05-15.

Core line:

> Corvus — See the winning move.

Corvus should sound:

- dark
- strategic
- observant
- precise
- premium
- ancient in restraint, not theatrical in excess

Use raven/oracle/omen language sparingly. It should sharpen the product, not make the interface harder to understand.

Approved candidate product names:

- Corvus — main product
- Omen — weekly best move
- The Pentatonic Scale — trade analyzer
- Sod — waiver recommendations
- Aerie — dashboard

Do not rename backend routes, database fields, packages, repositories, environment variables, domains, or deployment config for branding until an infrastructure migration plan is approved.

---

## Writing Rules

Use direct language.

Do not pad.

Do not hedge when a decision has been made.

Do not use filler.

Do not sound casual to the point of being flippant.

Do not sound like a chatbot.

Do not use empty startup language.

Do not over-explain obvious points.

Every sentence should carry weight.

---

## Product Language

Corvus should sound like:

- a serious fantasy football decision system
- a sharp coach
- a front office
- a trusted sports mind
- a product with a point of view

Corvus should not sound like:

- a generic AI assistant
- a gambling product
- a meme brand
- a corporate SaaS deck
- a chatbot
- a fantasy content farm

---

## Documentation Standard

Documentation should be clear enough for Justin to use and strict enough for Claude and Codex to follow.

Preferred structure:

1. decision
2. reason
3. rule
4. next action

Avoid long explanations unless the issue is complex or risky.

---

## Permanent Voice Rule

Institutional but human.

Direct.

Opinionated.

Never padded.

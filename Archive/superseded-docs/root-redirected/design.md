# Corvus Design Direction

## Product Name

Corvus

## Relationship

Slops Saloon is the umbrella brand.

Corvus is the active fantasy football product.

---

## Primary Phrase

Deus pascit corvos

## Supporting Line

Birds of a feather flock together.

---

## Design Goal

Dark, elegant, mythological, minimal, and trustworthy.

The product should feel premium but practical.

Do not let the theme overpower usability.

---

## Current Page Goals

### Corvus App

Build a polished app experience for:

- Draft Assistant
- MVP Move / Omen of the Week
- Trade Analyzer
- Start/Sit
- Waiver Wire
- platform connection states

### Slops Saloon Landing Page

Keep it lightweight.

It should introduce the umbrella brand and point users to Corvus.

Do not build a full media hub yet.

---

## Landing Page Structure

1. Header
   - Slops Saloon umbrella identity
   - Corvus feature link/card

2. Hero
   - Corvus as the first flagship product
   - Clear CTA to enter or learn about Corvus

3. Corvus Preview
   - Draft Assistant
   - MVP Move / Omen of the Week
   - Trade Analyzer

4. Coming Later
   - Keep vague and minimal
   - Do not add unrelated product detail

---

## Color Palette

- Raven Black: `#050505`
- Charcoal: `#141414`
- Antique Gold: `#C9A44C`
- Bone White: `#F4EFE1`
- Deep Crimson: `#6E1E2B`

---

## UI Standard

Every major feature should include:

- loading state
- error state
- empty state
- disconnected state if platform data matters
- clear action button
- understandable explanation text
- mobile-friendly layout

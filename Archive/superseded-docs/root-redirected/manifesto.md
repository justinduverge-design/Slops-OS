# Corvus Manifesto

## Mission

Corvus exists to help fantasy football players make sharper decisions.

The product should reduce confusion, surface useful context, and help users act with confidence.

Corvus is not a chatbot. It is a decision layer.

---

## Brand Relationship

Slops Saloon is the umbrella brand.

Corvus is the active product.

Slops Saloon can introduce the world. Corvus must deliver the value.

---

## Core Principle

Backbone before feature expansion.

A feature is not complete unless it fits into the shared app structure, uses consistent UI patterns, has clear API contracts, handles failure states, and supports a polished experience.

---

## Product Priorities

1. Stable app backbone
2. Draft Assistant as first-impression free tool
3. MVP Move / Omen of the Week as paid centerpiece
4. Supporting fantasy tools
5. Lightweight Slops Saloon landing page

---

## Non-Negotiables

- No general user-facing chatbot ships.
- No placeholder feature ships as if it is live.
- No paid MVP Move launch ships without the promised platform support.
- Yahoo, Sleeper, and ESPN are required for paid MVP Move launch.
- ESPN is launch-critical and security-critical.
- Secrets must never be exposed.
- Auth, payment, Supabase migrations, DNS, SSL, Docker, and VPS changes require explicit approval.
- No paid dependency is added without Justin approval.

---

## User Experience Standard

The app should feel:

- trustworthy
- clear
- fast enough
- polished
- mobile-friendly
- direct
- fantasy-football specific
- mythological without being confusing

The theme should support the product, not overwhelm it.

---

## Corvus Decision Style

Recommendations should be:

- explainable
- grounded in available data
- honest about uncertainty
- clear about risk
- clear about confidence
- useful even when data is incomplete

A good Corvus recommendation says:

```text
Here is the move.
Here is why it matters.
Here is the risk.
Here is how confident we are.
```

---

## Draft Assistant Doctrine

Draft Assistant is the first-impression tool.

It is free this year only.

It must feel polished enough to make users trust Corvus.

It must not be built as a one-off feature. It should reuse shared layouts, API patterns, and explanation components that can also support MVP Move.

---

## MVP Move Doctrine

MVP Move / Omen of the Week is the paid centerpiece.

It should feel like the weekly moment users return for.

It should not be rushed into paid launch before the platform backbone and data contracts are trustworthy.

---

## Scope Discipline

The product should not chase every good idea.

Future projects and future sport expansions should be parked unless Justin explicitly reactivates them.

A smaller product that works is better than an empire that never ships.

---

## Founder Rule

Justin owns the vision and final decision.

Claude and Codex should reduce confusion, protect focus, and move the product forward in small, safe, reversible steps.
